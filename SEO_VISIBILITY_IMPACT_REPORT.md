## 3. Establishing "Topical Authority" in Google

Google's modern search ranking systems (including the *Helpful Content System*, *RankBrain*, and *MUM*) rank websites based on **Topical Depth**:

1. **Semantic Search Clusters**: Google connects related concepts. When Adroit publishes connected content on *penthouses*, *commercial yields*, *builder floors*, *villas*, and *RERA laws*, Google's knowledge graph recognizes Adroit as a complete subject-matter authority on Delhi NCR real estate.
2. **Transfer of Link Equity (PageRank)**: Every blog post includes strategic internal links back to core commercial pages (`/`, `/weddings`, `/corporate-events`, `/social-events`, `/gifting`, `/contact`). This flows domain equity from informational readers directly to your lead-capture pages.
3. **Elevating Core Pages**: Because the domain's overall topical authority rises, the **homepage and primary service pages rank higher** for competitive queries than they could on their own.

## 5. Next-Generation AI Search Visibility (ChatGPT, Perplexity & AI Overviews)

Search behavior is shifting rapidly to conversational AI search engines:
* **Google Gemini / AI Overviews**
* **Perplexity AI**
* **ChatGPT Search / SearchGPT**

### How AI Search Differs from Traditional Search:
* AI search engines **never quote homepage marketing slogans**.
* AI models look for **structured, factual data**: specific price ranges, line-item budgets, regulatory clause citations, and venue comparison tables.
* By providing explicit numbers (e.g., *$125,000 to $750,000+ budget tiers for Atlanta South Asian weddings*, *7% to 9% pre-leased office yields*, *Section 14(3) RERA defect liability rules*), **your blog posts serve as the authoritative reference data that AI models cite directly in summary responses**.

## 6. Crawl Frequency & Indexing Velocity

```
[ Static Brochure Site ]  ──► Crawled once every 14 - 30 days  ──► Slow indexation of changes
[ Active Content Hub ]    ──► Crawled every 1 - 3 days         ──► Immediate discovery & fresh indexing
```

1. **Googlebot Crawl Budget**: Search engine bots allocate crawl time based on website activity. Regularly publishing comprehensive content signals to Googlebot that the domain is actively maintained.
2. **Freshness Algorithm**: Google applies a "Query Deserves Freshness" (QDF) filter to topics with changing market conditions (such as 2026 real estate pricing and wedding budgets). Fresh content earns an initial visibility boost.

4. **Phase 4: Authority Consolidation & Lead Inflow (Months 3 to 6)**:
   * Core articles reach **Top 3 to Top 5** rankings for competitive local keywords.
   * *Result*: Predictable, inbound commercial inquiries and consultation bookings.
